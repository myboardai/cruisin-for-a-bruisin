Born in San Ramon, CA

Forty years of band brothers

It started with three songs at a church talent show. We probably didn't have the most talent in the room, but the folks listening loved it, so we played those three songs over and over.

That was 40 years ago, and we've been making music together ever since. These days we don't call ourselves band members. We're band brothers.

We've played every kind of venue you can think of, and we'd love to make your next gathering cool, fun and nostalgic.
